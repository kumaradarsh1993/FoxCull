## New: Settings, rebuilt

The gear (or **⌘,** on a Mac, **Ctrl+,** on Windows) now opens Settings as
one window over the library, instead of a long list that ran off the screen.

- **Six sections** in a sidebar: Appearance, Playback, Speed & storage, Files
  & catalog, Controls, About & updates. Every setting says what it does right
  under its name.
- **Search** at the top finds any setting by what you'd call it ("dark",
  "delete", "sd card", "controller"), and you can change it right there in
  the results.
- **Themes** show a small preview of each one. On/off settings are switches.
- **Prepare is back in plain sight**: Settings → Speed & storage → *Prepare
  this folder* builds every preview of the open folder ahead of time (worth
  it on SD cards and slow disks), with a progress bar and Stop. It keeps
  going if you close Settings. Next to it: how much space this drive's
  previews take. The folder's right-click menu still has it too.
- **Excluded folders** and **Game controller** open as pages inside Settings,
  with a back link (or Esc). The mouse's thumb buttons moved to Controls.
- **About & updates** is the last section; the gear shows a dot when a newer
  build is out.
