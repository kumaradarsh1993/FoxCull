## Redesign, part 3 of 3: Focus, Details, menus, Merge and Reel

- **Focus:**
  - **Tab** hides or shows the sidebar, as in Lightroom;
  - the play button is smaller and calmer;
  - the area around the picture follows Settings → Behind the pictures.
- **Details:** the file name gets the room. FPS and Codec are off by default (turn them
  on under Columns), so Size and Date fit on a laptop screen.
- **Right-click menus** start with what you do most (Open in Focus, Pick, Reject, Edit,
  Merge, Reel) and end with Previous and Next.
- **Less flicker:** the bottom-left status only mentions thumbnails being made when it
  takes longer than a second.
- **Reel:** a clear two-step header and a taller waveform for choosing the part of the
  song.
- **Small labels** across Merge, Reel, Details and the job list are in normal sentence case
  instead of capitals, matching Settings and Edit.
