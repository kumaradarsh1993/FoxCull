One fix for Macs.

- **The filmstrip scrolls the way your fingers move.** On a trackpad or Magic
  Mouse, swiping sideways moved the strip the wrong way, and every swipe was
  re-animated so the strip lagged behind and drifted after you let go. Now a
  sideways swipe scrolls the Mac's own way, with its momentum and a bounce at
  the ends, and an up/down swipe moves the strip along by the same amount,
  straight away. Windows behaves as before.
