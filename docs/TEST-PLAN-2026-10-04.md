# What to test: everything since 2026-10-03 night (nightly.3 → nightly.12)

The owner's checklist for testing the nightlies built on 2026-10-04 in one go,
on the latest build (`v1.5.2-nightly.13`; the redesign is .10–.12, section H; .13 fixes
the update check, section I). Every item is built and checked in
the browser harness (and real ffmpeg where noted), but **none of it has been
used in the real app yet**: that is what this list is for. Tick items off; a
line saying what went wrong is enough for the next session to act on.

Ledgers with the details: `docs/changes/2026-10-04-*.md`.

Items marked **⚠** are the ones most likely to behave differently in the real
app than in the harness.

## A. Library (nightly.3)

- [ ] **Drag picture:** select several items and drag them over a sidebar folder. You
      should see a small stack with a count, and the folder says "Move N" or "Copy N"
      (hold ⌥ to copy).
- [ ] **Move within one drive:** instant; ratings and labels stay on the files.
- [ ] **Move to another drive** (SSD → Mac or the other way):
      - a job bottom-left shows size, speed, time left and Stop;
      - the marks arrive with the files;
      - the originals are deleted only after the copy is checked.
- [ ] **Two drops in a row:** the second one queues; Stop works on a queued move too.
- [ ] **Job centre** (bottom-left): several jobs at once; click to expand; "Recent";
      finished moves offer "Open folder".
- [ ] **Missing items:**
      - right-click a folder → "Remove N missing items…";
      - a mixed selection offers "Select all N missing here" and "Remove the 3 missing
        of these 12…";
      - relinking one file reconnects its neighbours.
- [ ] **Group by Day** (Arrange): evening clips stay on their own day.
- [ ] **Pick/reject tabs:** a green tab on top of picked tiles, a red one under rejected
      ones.
- [ ] **Spring-loaded folders:** hold a drag over a closed folder for about 0.7 s and it
      opens.

## B. Edit and Merge in their own windows (nightly.4)

- [ ] **Edit opens as its own window** (toolbar Edit, **E**, or right-click → "Add to the
      Edit timeline"); the library stays as it is.
- [ ] **Clips into Edit:**
      - **⚠ dragging from the library window into the Edit window** (if this fails,
        **E** and **⌘C** in the library → **⌘V** in Edit are the fallbacks);
      - clips with ✂ ranges arrive as separate pieces.
- [ ] **Side by side:** the library goes left and Edit right.
- [ ] **≠ badge and a notice** when a clip doesn't match the timeline (a vertical clip
      in a landscape edit, say).
- [ ] **Close and reopen Edit:** the timeline comes back. With a drive unplugged, its
      clips are dashed and named.
- [ ] **Merge runs in its own window:**
      - start a merge, then close the window: it carries on in the job centre, and
        "Show merge window" brings it back;
      - **Pause / Resume / Stop** (Stop asks twice);
      - when done, "Show in folder".
- [ ] **⌘Q during a merge, export or copy** asks first; no half-written files are left.
- [ ] **Errors in words:** a full disk, or a card pulled out mid-merge.

## C. Segments: several in/out pieces per clip (nightly.5)

- [ ] **Marking in Focus** on a video:
      - **[** marks an in and **]** an out;
      - pressing either again moves it;
      - you can have several pieces, and they never overlap.
- [ ] **Pieces on the scrub bar** show as highlighted spans:
      - drag a marker frame by frame;
      - click a marker, then ←/→ nudges it;
      - right-click removes an in, an out, a piece, or all.
- [ ] **Drag one piece** (or all of them) to Edit.
- [ ] **Merge with pieces:**
      - a Segments column with a checkbox per clip (ticked by default) and a header
        checkbox for all;
      - the totals follow the checkboxes;
      - the preview starts at the first in and plays only the ticked pieces, and the
        scrub bar works;
      - the row menu has "Use the whole clip" and "Show in library".
- [ ] **Lossless merge with pieces:** each piece starts on a keyframe (up to ~0.5 s early
      on the Osmo). **Convert** cuts exactly.

## D. Reel: clips cut to the beat (nightly.5, .6)

- [ ] **Open it:** select clips → right-click → "Create a reel synced to music…".
- [ ] **⚠ Song:** drop a song from Finder onto the Reel window (or use Choose a song).
- [ ] **⚠ Beats on real music:** try a pop song and a calm one. Do the marks sit on the
      beats, and the big ones on the start of each bar?
- [ ] **Section of the song:** drag it on the waveform. Its edges snap to bar starts (⌥
      for free); zoom; click to listen.
- [ ] **Beat board:**
      - drag a window to change which part of a clip plays;
      - drag its right edge to cut on a different beat, and the later clips re-flow;
      - a clip too short for its window shows red, and "Fix for me" solves it;
      - hovering a strip shows that frame;
      - **⚠ Play the reel** (Space): is it smooth on 4K clips?
- [ ] **Export reel:** a 1080×1920 file with the song from the chosen point, fading out
      at the end. Do the cuts land on the beats?
- [ ] **New song on step 2:** dropping one goes back to step 1 (fixed in .6).
- [ ] **Close and reopen the Reel window:** the reel comes back.

## Open items: decisions and ideas waiting on the owner

1. **Edit vs Reel:** the Edit button sends whatever is selected in the library, so after
   making a reel, Edit opened with the same clips. Should Edit open empty unless clips
   are sent on purpose? The owner leaned towards keeping them separate.
2. **Overlapping clips:** the top row (V1) shows over V2. Premiere does the opposite (V2
   over V1). Which one?
3. **"Export without music"** (proposed, not built): for songs licensed only inside
   Instagram. FoxCull cuts to your copy of the song, exports silent, and tells you
   where in the song to start when you add it in Instagram.
4. **Choosing the crop of landscape clips in a reel:** in the spec, for later.
5. **Faster merges:** a custom MP4 join that reads each file in one pass might reach the
   disk's plain read speed. Noted; large and risky.
6. **Stable:** the latest stable is v1.5.1. v1.5.2 becomes stable only when the owner
   says "ship it".
7. **Free music for reels:** licensed songs can't be brought in. Free sources if wanted
   later: Openverse (CC audio search API), Jamendo (free API for non-commercial use,
   credit the artist), Musopen (public-domain classical recordings).
